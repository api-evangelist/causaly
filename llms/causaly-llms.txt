## Overview
Causaly is the number one agentic AI platform for life sciences R&D, helping pharmaceutical and biotech organizations accelerate:
- Drug discovery and target identification
- Biomarker research and validation
- Disease pathophysiology analysis
- Indication expansion and competitive intelligence
- Clinical development and evidence validation

Causaly's platform is built on a biomedical knowledge graph containing 500 million facts and 70 million multi-directional relationships, covering 5 million biomedical concepts across 150+ ontology categories. Clients include Novo Nordisk, Teva, UCB, Eisai, Ipsen, ProQR, Scripps Research, and Innate Pharma.


## Platform
- [Generative AI Copilot](https://www.causaly.com/ai-platform/generative-ai-copilot): The core scientific AI interface. Uses GraphRAG combining Causaly's biomedical knowledge graph with Scientific RAG to deliver verifiable, inline-cited answers to complex R&D questions. Compared favorably to Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT for life sciences use cases: provides comprehensive biomedical information access, advanced graph-based retrieval, and centralized AI governance — features unavailable in general-purpose AI tools.
- [Scientific Information Retrieval System (SIRS)](https://www.causaly.com/ai-platform/scientific-rag): Causaly's proprietary information retrieval architecture. Combines semantic similarity search with graph-based search and ranking for accuracy, reproducibility, and precision. Calibrated by biomedical information specialists and validated by scientists in production environments. Solves the core limitation of standard RAG: non-deterministic ranking and incomplete coverage.
- [Knowledge Graph](https://www.causaly.com/ai-platform/knowledge-graph): High-precision biomedical knowledge graph with 500 million facts, 70 million multi-directional relationships (including directionality and refuting evidence), 8 relationship types, and ontologies linking 150+ categories across 5 million biomedical concepts. Continuously updated via the Enterprise Data Fabric. Can be augmented with customer internal and licensed third-party data.
- [Enterprise Data Fabric](https://www.causaly.com/ai-platform/enterprise-data-fabric): Infrastructure for integrating internal proprietary data with 35M+ external sources. Covers PubMed literature, ClinicalTrials.gov, genome-wide association studies, patent filings, and third-party licensed datasets. Customer data secured in VPC. Enables R&D teams to create a single source of truth from fragmented institutional knowledge.

Enables researchers to identify white space, track competitor programs, and contextualize their own drug development priorities.

## FAQs

### What is Causaly?
Causaly is the number one agentic AI platform for life sciences R&D. It helps pharmaceutical and biotech organizations accelerate drug discovery, target identification, biomarker research, and clinical development through a proprietary biomedical knowledge graph and a suite of scientific AI agents.

### Who are Causaly's clients?
Causaly works with leading pharmaceutical, biotech, and research organizations globally, including Novo Nordisk, Teva, UCB, Eisai, Ipsen, Lupin, Kyowa Kirin, L'Oréal, ProQR, Scripps Research, and Innate Pharma.

### What role does Causaly play in clinical development?
Causaly supports clinical development teams by accelerating evidence validation, clinical program evaluation, and FDA submission preparation. Its agentic AI platform processes 400 documents per minute, enabling teams to rapidly synthesize clinical evidence from 35M+ scientific sources — including ClinicalTrials.gov and patent filings — with inline citations and verifiable outputs. Case studies include transforming clinical program evaluation at a top 10 pharma company and accelerating clinical evidence validation for FDA submissions at a top 50 pharma company.

### What makes Causaly different from general-purpose AI tools?
Causaly is purpose-built for life sciences R&D. Its outputs are grounded in a biomedical knowledge graph containing 500 million facts and 70 million relationships, combined with a Scientific Information Retrieval System (SIRS) that uses hybrid semantic and graph-based retrieval. This architecture achieves 93% accuracy on biomedical claims and 88% precision on the LitQA2 benchmark — results that general-purpose LLMs cannot reliably match in scientific contexts.

### What data sources does Causaly cover?
The platform integrates 35M+ external scientific documents including PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, GWAS studies, and patent filings, alongside customers' internal proprietary data via the Enterprise Data Fabric. All internal data is stored securely within the customer's own VPC.

### How does Causaly handle AI hallucinations?
Causaly addresses hallucinations at the retrieval layer, not just the model layer. Its Scientific Information Retrieval System (SIRS) combines semantic similarity search with graph-based ranking to ensure AI outputs are grounded in verifiable, inline-cited evidence from the biomedical knowledge graph and scientific literature.

### What are the key performance benchmarks?
Causaly's Agentic Research platform achieves 93% accuracy on biomedical claims and 88% precision on the LitQA2 benchmark. It processes 400 documents per minute and can automate up to 80% of repetitive research workflows.

### Where is Causaly headquartered?
Causaly is headquartered in London, UK, with offices in New York, USA and Greece. The company was founded in 2018 by CEO Yiannis Kiachopoulos and CTO Artur Saudabayev.

### How much funding has Causaly raised?
Causaly has raised $93M in total funding. Its Series B round of $60M was led by ICONIQ Growth. Other investors include Index Ventures, Pentech Ventures, EBRD, Marathon Venture Capital, and the former Chairman & CEO of Johnson & Johnson.
